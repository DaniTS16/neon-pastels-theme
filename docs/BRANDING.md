# Branding and Brand Lock — Neon Pastels

## Brand Owner

- **Parent company (legal reference only):** IRI National  
- **Public brand:** Luv Media Group  
- **Required legal copy:** IRI National d/b/a Luv Media Group  

This theme is designed to ensure that all public-facing deployments accurately represent the Luv Media Group brand identity.

---

## Brand Lock Rules

Brand lock is a strict enforcement layer that prevents unauthorized or accidental modification of brand-critical elements.

### The following actions are **not allowed**

- Overriding or renaming the public brand
- Displaying the parent company as a public-facing brand
- Removing or altering the required legal footer
- Removing or altering color palettes
- Overriding motion specifications outside theme options

These rules ensure that the theme remains compliant with brand identity and legal requirements.

---

## Private Luv-Only Mode

Legal footer removal is permitted **only** when:

- `private_mode.enabled = true`  
- `LuvOnlyMode = true`  

This mode is intended for:

- Internal demos  
- Portfolio presentations  
- Non-public previews  

---

## Notes

Brand lock is not a restriction — it is a guarantee of consistency.  
It ensures that every public-facing instance of Neon Pastels reflects the identity, values, and legal obligations of Luv Media Group.
